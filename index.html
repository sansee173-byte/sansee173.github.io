← → run  |  ↑ look up  |  ↓ crouch / roll
Space or Z: jump  |  hold X: Peel Out, release to dash
↓ + jump (tap to rev), release ↓: Spin Dash
In the air press ↓: Drop Dash. Land holding jump, release to dash
